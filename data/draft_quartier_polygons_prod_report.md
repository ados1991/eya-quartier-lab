# Draft quartier polygons v3 — FULL LAND COVERAGE

## Method

Every non-water land cell inside Cotonou + Abomey-Calavi is assigned to the quartier of its nearest owned road vertex. Arrondissement boundaries are NOT used as hard limits (only communes are). Grid: 20 m. Water exclusion: major water polygons + waterways buffered by 12 m. Simplification: Douglas-Peucker 6 m. Holes < 500 m² filled; slivers < 200 m² dropped.
