## Methodology

**Per-quartier scores** (for every chef-lieu Q):
- `support_inside`  POIs whose label matches Q and whose nearest road node is currently owned by Q.
- `contradict_inside` POIs in Q's territory whose label points to a different chef.
- `claim_outside`  POIs labelled for Q whose nearest road node is currently owned by someone else.
- `wrongness_score = (contradict_inside + claim_outside) / (support_inside + 1)`

**Territorial evidence zones**:
- Single-link spatial cluster of disputed POIs (eps 250 m) sharing the same (from → to) flip.
- `affected_road_length_m` = sum of edge lengths inside the zone's convex hull + 100 m buffer where both endpoints are currently owned by the `from` chef.
- Confidence: HIGH = ≥ 4 POIs AND road ≥ 200 m; MEDIUM = ≥ 3 POIs OR (≥ 2 POIs AND road ≥ 300 m); LOW otherwise.
